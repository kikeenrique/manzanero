# Feature status

This page is the compact inventory of what is available now and how far each area has been checked. “Verified” always means a scoped live result on the recorded device and environment; it is not a general reliability guarantee. [Verification](verification.md) holds the evidence and the failures, the [roadmap](roadmap.md) orders the remaining work, and the [live check plan](live-check-plan.md) lists the hardware runs still owed.

Unless a row says otherwise, physical evidence comes from one iPhone 13 mini (iOS 27.2.1) and simulator evidence from iOS 27.2 runtime simulators.

## Unavailable or missing

- Simulator `wake`: refused as not implemented (`unsupported-operation`). Simulator `paste` is refused because `simctl pbcopy` did not reach the pasteboard. `type` of text the named layout cannot produce is refused in favour of `set-value`, on both platforms.
- Physical `logs`: refused as `unsupported-operation` until the native `os_trace_relay` route exists ([app logs](app-logs.md)).
- `hid-info`, live raw `ax-snapshot`, independent Wi-Fi pairing (`pair wifi`) and `--pairing-file` are no longer commands. Pair through Xcode/CoreDevice; offline `decode-ax-archive` still works.
- The Apple TV remote's touch surface ([blocked](other-families.md#remote-touch-surface-not-built)).
- Web content on a phone: with a web page in front in Chrome, `tree` and every selector action failed with "snapshot root identity mismatch" ([session](verification.md#phone-session-of-2026-11-17)). Touch, keys, screenshots and OCR are unaffected.
- Elements with neither label nor identifier, such as the Settings search field, cannot be selected by `press`, `scroll`, `wait` or `set-value`.

Those retired routes used to reach AXe, idevice or ipb helpers. The CLI no longer contains them (see [retired helpers](dependencies.md#retired-helpers)), and the execution policy would refuse those binaries anyway.

User-facing live mirroring, reusable semantic element handles, automatic permission/Face ID handling, crash recovery, device-side release after an uncertain one and a complete assertion system are missing or incomplete.

## Apple-family coverage

| Family | Current scope |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone | Primary physical target; evidence from one iPhone 13 mini in portrait. No second phone and no landscape on hardware |
| iPad | Simulator only: one iPad mini simulator in portrait and landscape. No physical iPad; multitasking and windowed apps untested |
| Apple Watch | One watchOS 27.2 simulator: `crown` (calibrated) and `button side` verified; `button action` verified on one Apple Watch Ultra simulator; trees, waits, press, tap and `button home` work. `key` and `type` are refused: keyboard events do not arrive, and `set-value` changes the field's text but not the app's bound value. No physical watch |
| Apple TV | One tvOS 27.2 simulator: `remote` (D-pad, select, menu, home, play-pause) verified with Settings and a probe app; trees mark the focused node and `wait --until focused` waits for focus; touch is refused; press, keys and typing work. Remote touch surface blocked; no physical Apple TV |
| iPhone Duo | One iOS 27.2 simulator: `fold`, `unfold` and `hinge` verified, with a derived posture; captures take `--display`. Touch, trees, actions, waits and default captures follow the active display, verified unfolded with the probe app (one to five contacts) and Settings. Home Screen tree empty (SpringBoard answers no snapshot at any depth); no physical device |

Accepting an identifier or passing a build does not establish support for a new family.

## How to read progress

Use three separate questions: is there an executable route, has that route been checked on this hardware, and does it recover from interruption/failure? A feature can answer yes to the first two and still be unsuitable for unattended use. No percentage is assigned, because counting commands would hide differences in scope and evidence.
